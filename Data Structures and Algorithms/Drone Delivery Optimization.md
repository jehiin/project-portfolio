# Drone Delivery Optimization

## Overview

Built a multi-mode routing engine for constrained drone-delivery networks using graph and combinatorial optimization algorithms.  
Implemented Prim's minimum spanning tree, a fast insertion-based traveling-salesperson heuristic, and an exact branch-and-bound solver.  
Combined geometric constraints, lower-bound pruning, and strong initial tours to reduce the search space while preserving optimality in exact mode.

## What This Demonstrates

- Minimum spanning trees
- Heuristics
- Branch-and-bound optimization
