# Stock Market Simulator

## Overview

Built an event-driven stock-market simulator that matches buy and sell orders by price-time priority.  
Combined custom priority queues with streaming median tracking, per-trader accounting, verbose trade reporting, and hindsight-optimal time-traveler analysis.  
Designed modular parsing and market-state components, then stress-tested ordering ties, partial fills, multiple equities, and generated input streams.

## What This Demonstrates

- Priority queues
- Streaming statistics
- Event-driven simulation
