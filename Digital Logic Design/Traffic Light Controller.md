# Traffic Light Controller

## Overview

Designed a six-state traffic-light controller for a multi-direction intersection with vehicle-request inputs and timed green/yellow phases.  
Combined a finite-state machine, countdown logic, clock division, and seven-segment displays into a complete FPGA-oriented system.  
Verified reset behavior, every approach combination, phase timing, and safe light transitions with a long-running testbench.

## What This Demonstrates

- Finite-state machines
- Clock division
- Timing and verification
